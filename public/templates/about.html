About Nur Al-Huda School Management System

Nur Al-Huda School Management System (pslms) is a comprehensive, intuitive educational management platform tailored for schools to seamlessly coordinate students, teachers, parents, and administrative staff.

Our Mission

Empowering educators, students, and parents through reliable, transparent, and user-friendly digital tools that make learning and school administration seamless.

Our Values

Excellence, transparency, and accessibility. We believe technology should simplify education and connect communities.

Roles & Capabilities

  • Admin: Complete oversight, student & teacher records, course assignment, resource management, and system configuration.
  • Teacher: Manage assigned classes, create assessments, grade students, compute ranks, and share learning materials.
  • Student: View enrolled subjects, assessment scores, overall averages, and download study materials and assignments.
  • Parent: Stay informed on children's academic performance, grades, report cards, and school resources.
